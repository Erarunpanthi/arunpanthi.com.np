Find the missing number: 3, 6, 11, 18, ?, 38
a) 25
b) 27
c) 29
d) 31

b) 27
Explanation: The pattern is +3, +5, +7, +9, +11. So, 18 + 9 = 27.

Complete the series: 2, 6, 12, 20, 30, ?
a) 40
b) 42
c) 44
d) 48

b) 42
Explanation: The pattern is +4, +6, +8, +10, +12. So, 30 + 12 = 42.

What number replaces the question mark? 5, 10, 17, 26, ?
a) 35
b) 37
c) 39
d) 41

b) 37
Explanation: The pattern is +5, +7, +9, +11. So, 26 + 11 = 37.

Find the missing number: 1, 8, 27, 64, ?, 216
a) 100
b) 125
c) 144
d) 169

b) 125
Explanation: This is a sequence of cubes (1³, 2³, 3³, 4³, 5³, 6³). So, 5³ = 125.

Which number completes the pattern? 7, 14, 28, 56, ?
a) 84
b) 96
c) 112
d) 120

c) 112
Explanation: Each number is multiplied by 2. So, 56 × 2 = 112.
