Choose the odd one out:
a) Triangle
b) Square
c) Circle
d) Rectangle

c) Circle
Explanation: Triangle, Square, and Rectangle are polygons with straight sides, while Circle is a curved shape.

Which shape does not belong?
a) Cube
b) Sphere
c) Cylinder
d) Triangle

d) Triangle
Explanation: Cube, Sphere, and Cylinder are 3D shapes, while Triangle is a 2D shape.

Identify the shape with all sides equal:
a) Rectangle
b) Rhombus
c) Trapezoid
d) Parallelogram

b) Rhombus
Explanation: A rhombus has all four sides equal in length.

Which polygon has the most sides?
a) Pentagon
b) Hexagon
c) Heptagon
d) Octagon

d) Octagon
Explanation: An octagon has 8 sides, which is more than pentagon (5), hexagon (6), and heptagon (7).

How many faces does a cube have?
a) 4
b) 6
c) 8
d) 12

b) 6
Explanation: A cube has 6 square faces.
