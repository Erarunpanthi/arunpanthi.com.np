Governance System

Which is the largest rural municipality in Nepal in terms of the number of wards?
A. Gajuri Rural Municipality
B. Putha Uttarganga Rural Municipality
C. Bhume Rural Municipality
D. Sisne Rural Municipality

B. Putha Uttarganga Rural Municipality

When did the World Bank introduce the concept of good governance?
A. 1987 AD
B. 1989 AD
C. 1990 AD
D. 1992 AD

B. 1989 AD

Which of the following provinces does NOT have any Sub-Metropolitan City?
A. Koshi
B. Gandaki
C. Lumbini
D. Bagmati

B. Gandaki

Which body is established by the Constitution of Nepal to resolve political disputes between the Federal Government and the Provinces, and among the Provinces?
A. Constitutional Council
B. Inter-Province Council
C. Provincial Coordination Council
D. National Development Council

B. Inter-Province Council

The meetings of rural municipal assembly and municipal assembly are usually held ………. a year.
A. Once
B. Twice
C. Three
D. Four

B. Twice

Which pair of metropolitan cities in Nepal has an equal number of wards?
A. Pokhara and Bharatpur
B. Pokhara and Kathmandu
C. Kathmandu and Birgunj
D. Lalitpur and Pokhara

C. Kathmandu and Birgunj

What is the Parliament of Bhutan officially known as?
A. Majlis
B. Pashtun
C. Dzongkha
D. Tshogdu

D. Tshogdu

Under which section of the good governance Act, 2064 did the Government of Nepal exercise its authority to formulate the Good Governance Rules, 2065?
A. Section 53
B. Section 45
C. Section 39
D. Section 32

D. Section 32

The Chairman/Mayor of local level take his/her oath of office in front of:
A. Judges of district court
B. Judges of supreme court
C. Chief Justice
D. Head of Province

A. Judges of district court
