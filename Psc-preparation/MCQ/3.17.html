If all Bloops are Razzies and some Razzies are Lazzies, then:
a) All Bloops are Lazzies
b) Some Bloops are Lazzies
c) No Bloops are Lazzies
d) None of the above

d) None of the above
Explanation: We cannot conclude that any Bloops are Lazzies. Some Razzies are Lazzies, but we don't know if those Razzies are also Bloops.

All cats are mammals. All mammals are animals. Therefore:
a) All cats are animals
b) All animals are cats
c) Some animals are not cats
d) Both a and c

d) Both a and c
Explanation: Since all cats are mammals and all mammals are animals, all cats are animals. Also, since there are many animals that are not cats, some animals are not cats.

No dogs are cats. Some pets are dogs. Therefore:
a) Some pets are not cats
b) All pets are cats
c) No pets are cats
d) Some cats are pets

a) Some pets are not cats
Explanation: Since some pets are dogs and no dogs are cats, those pets that are dogs cannot be cats.

All engineers are graduates. Some graduates are teachers. Which conclusion follows?
a) All engineers are teachers
b) Some engineers are teachers
c) No engineers are teachers
d) None of the above

d) None of the above
Explanation: We cannot determine the relationship between engineers and teachers from the given statements.

All roses are flowers. Some flowers fade quickly. Therefore:
a) All roses fade quickly
b) Some roses fade quickly
c) No roses fade quickly
d) None of the above

d) None of the above
Explanation: We cannot conclude anything specific about roses fading quickly just because some flowers do.
