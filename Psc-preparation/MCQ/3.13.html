Which word does not belong to the group?
a) Apple
b) Banana
c) Carrot
d) Orange

c) Carrot
Explanation: Apple, Banana, and Orange are fruits, while Carrot is a vegetable.

Choose the odd one out:
a) Triangle
b) Square
c) Circle
d) Rectangle

c) Circle
Explanation: Triangle, Square, and Rectangle are polygons with straight sides, while Circle is a curved shape.

Find the odd one out:
a) Dog
b) Cat
c) Lion
d) Eagle

d) Eagle
Explanation: Dog, Cat, and Lion are mammals, while Eagle is a bird.

Which number is different from the others?
a) 4
b) 9
c) 16
d) 21

d) 21
Explanation: 4, 9, and 16 are perfect squares (2², 3², 4²), while 21 is not a perfect square.

Identify the odd one:
a) Mumbai
b) Delhi
c) Kolkata
d) Nepal

d) Nepal
Explanation: Mumbai, Delhi, and Kolkata are cities in India, while Nepal is a country.
