How it works

  • Every Wednesday a new test card appears above automatically, labelled with that Wednesday's Nepali (BS) date — no page edit needed.
  • Click Give Exam — the exam runs like the other PSC tests: full-screen timed mode, question palette, warning for unanswered questions, and auto-submit when time ends.
  • After submission you get your score, percentage, time taken and a full answer review. As in the other tests, a submitted test cannot be retaken.
  • Missed a week? All previous Wednesday tests remain in the archive above for revision.
