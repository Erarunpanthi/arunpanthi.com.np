If 5 workers can complete a task in 12 days, how many days will 10 workers take?
a) 4 days
b) 6 days
c) 8 days
d) 10 days

b) 6 days
Explanation: Workers and days are inversely proportional. 5 × 12 = 10 × x, so x = 6 days.

A can do a work in 10 days and B can do the same work in 15 days. How many days will they take working together?
a) 5 days
b) 6 days
c) 7 days
d) 8 days

b) 6 days
Explanation: A's 1 day work = 1/10, B's 1 day work = 1/15. Together = 1/10 + 1/15 = 1/6. So, 6 days.

If 8 men can build a wall in 20 days, how many men are needed to build it in 10 days?
a) 12
b) 14
c) 16
d) 18

c) 16
Explanation: Men and days are inversely proportional. 8 × 20 = x × 10, so x = 16 men.

A pipe can fill a tank in 4 hours. Another pipe can empty it in 6 hours. If both pipes are opened together, how long will it take to fill the tank?
a) 8 hours
b) 10 hours
c) 12 hours
d) 14 hours

c) 12 hours
Explanation: Filling rate = 1/4, Emptying rate = 1/6. Net rate = 1/4 - 1/6 = 1/12. So, 12 hours.

12 workers can complete a job in 8 days. After 3 days, 4 workers leave. How many more days will the remaining workers take to complete the job?
a) 5 days
b) 6 days
c) 7 days
d) 7.5 days

d) 7.5 days
Explanation: Total work = 12 × 8 = 96 worker-days. Work done in 3 days = 12 × 3 = 36. Remaining = 60. With 8 workers: 60/8 = 7.5 days.
