NEC License Exam Model Test Sets

Frequently Asked Questions

What are NEC Model Test Sets?

They are practice tests structured like the actual NEC (Nepal Engineering Council) license exam for civil engineers, helping you prepare under timed exam conditions.

How are the sets different from each other?

Each set contains a fresh mix of questions covering construction materials, soil mechanics, surveying, structural analysis, RCC design, hydraulics, irrigation, transportation, estimation and construction management.

Can I see my score?

Yes, each test shows your score, percentage and detailed solutions immediately after submission.
