What is the PSC Civil Engineering Exam Syllabus?

The PSC Civil Engineering syllabus covers Structural Engineering, Geotechnical Engineering, Water Resources, Transportation, Construction Management, and General Knowledge sections as prescribed by the Nepal Public Service Commission (Lok Sewa Aayog). You can read the full, updated syllabus on our PSC Syllabus page.

CivPrepMaster provides free PSC Civil Engineering notes, past questions, MCQ practice tests, and recommended book lists to help you prepare for the Lok Sewa Aayog exam. Start with the First Paper notes and the Second Paper past questions.

How do I prepare for the Nepal PSC Civil Engineering exam?

Start by reviewing the updated PSC syllabus, study recommended books, practice past questions, take subject-wise MCQ tests, and watch preparation videos available on CivPrepMaster. Use the exam timer and Pomodoro tools to build real exam discipline.
