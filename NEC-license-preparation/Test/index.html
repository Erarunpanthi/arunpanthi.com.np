Frequently asked questions about the NEC license practice tests

Are these official NEC Civil Engineering License Exam papers?

No. These are independent practice tests for study and revision. They are not official Nepal Engineering Council exam papers or endorsed by the NEC. Check current official notices and syllabus for exam requirements.

How many NEC civil engineering model test sets are available?

There are five timed model test sets. Each set focuses on a different mix of civil engineering subjects.

Can I see my score after submitting a test?

Yes. After submission, the test displays your score, percentage, and the correct answer for each question.
