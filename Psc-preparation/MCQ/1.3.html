Which one is wrong from the following options?
A. Rukum east is hilly area
B. Myagdi is mountainous district
C. Makawanpur is inner terai
D. Terhathum is hilly district

A. Rukum east is hilly area

Where is Coniferous forests seen in Nepal?
A. Chure, Bhawar Region
B. Hilly Region
C. Mahabharat Hill Region
D. Mountain Region

C. Mahabharat Hill Region

Harishayani Ekadasi falls in?
A. Kartik Shukla Paksha
B. Kartik Krishna Paksha
C. Bala Chaturdasi
D. Krishna Chaturdasi

A. Kartik Shukla Paksha

Which geographical region of Nepal has the most suitable climate for human health?
A. Chure Range
B. High Himalayan Region
C. Mahabharat Range
D. Bhabar Region

C. Mahabharat Range
