If BOOK is coded as CPPL, how is PEN coded?
a) QFO
b) QDO
c) ODM
d) QEN

b) QDO
Explanation: Each letter is shifted by +1, -1, +1, -1 pattern. P(+1)=Q, E(-1)=D, N(+1)=O. So, PEN becomes QDO.

If CAT is coded as DBU, how is DOG coded?
a) EPH
b) FQH
c) EPJ
d) FPI

a) EPH
Explanation: Each letter is shifted by +1. D+1=E, O+1=P, G+1=H. So, DOG becomes EPH.

In a certain code, TREE is written as USFF. How is LEAF written?
a) MFBG
b) MFBH
c) NFCH
d) MEAG

a) MFBG
Explanation: Each letter is shifted by +1. L+1=M, E+1=F, A+1=B, F+1=G. So, LEAF becomes MFBG.

If WATER is coded as XBUFS, how is FIRE coded?
a) GJSF
b) GJSE
c) HJTF
d) GJSD

a) GJSF
Explanation: Each letter is shifted by +1. F+1=G, I+1=J, R+1=S, E+1=F. So, FIRE becomes GJSF.

If APPLE is coded as ZOOKD, how is BANANA coded?
a) AZMZMZ
b) ZYLMZM
c) AZLZMZ
d) ZYKZLZ

a) AZMZMZ
Explanation: Each letter is replaced by its opposite in the alphabet (A↔Z, B↔Y, etc.). B↔A, A↔Z, N↔M, A↔Z, N↔M, A↔Z. So, BANANA becomes AZMZMZ.
