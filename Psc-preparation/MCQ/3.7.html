Estimating, Costing, Specification and Valuation

The number of bricks required for a wall of 80' long, 1'3" thick and 8" high with one mortar joint is (The dimension of brick is 10"*5"*3")
A. 7000
B. 8000
C. 8064
D. 9000

C. 8064

The information that cannot be included in drawings is conveyed to the estimator through through
A. specification
B. cover note
C. progress chart
D. none of these

A. specification

The volume of 1 bag of cement is….... M^3
A. 0.067
B. 0.05
C. 0.033
D. 0.025

C. 0.033

In the estimation of plastering, surface the deductions are not made for,
A. ends of beams
B. end of rafters
C. small openings up to 0.5 sq m
D. all of the above

D. all of the above

The quantity of damp proofing curse (DPC) is worked out in
A. meter
B. square meter
C. cubic meter
D. lump sum

B. square meter

The process of finding the fair price of property is
A. pricing
B. costing
C. valuation
D. estimation

C. valuation

Bar-bed wire is measured in
A. running meters
B. gauge
C. weight
D. both a) and b) above

A. running meters

The quantity of works of G.I. ridge of a sloped roof is calculated in
A. cubic meter
B. square meter
C. running meter
D. kg

C. running meter

Which of the following is not a part of estimating norms for rate analysis
A. Tax
B. labour cost
C. Material cost
D. None of above

A. Tax

Specification for holdfast are given in terms of
A. number
B. volume
C. weight
D. length

C. weight

In long and short wall method of estimation, the length of long wall is the centre to centre distance between the walls and
A. breadth of the wall
B. one fourth breadth of wall on each side
C. half breadth of wall on each side
D. none of above

C. half breadth of wall on each side

The supplementary specification prepared and applied if the standard specification does not cover the requirement of works under a contract is called
A. general specification
B. contract specification
C. performance specification
D. special specification

D. special specification

Plinth area rate method of estimation is generally used for
A. Detailed estimates
B. Revised estimates
C. Approximate estimates
D. Supplementary estimates

C. Approximate estimates

A supplementary estimate is prepared when:
A. Work is to be done without contract
B. Original estimate has some technical errors
C. There's an increase in the quantity of work
D. Rates have changed in the market

C. There's an increase in the quantity of work

Depreciation is calculated on:
A. Original cost of construction
B. Salvage value
C. Cost of land
D. Cost of structure or equipment over time

D. Cost of structure or equipment over time

Which of the following items is usually not estimated in lump sum?
A. Boundary wall
B. Site clearance
C. Earthwork in excavation
D. Water supply and sanitation

C. Earthwork in excavation

The daily wage of skilled labor is considered in
A. Lump sum basis
B. Based on output per day
C. Based on output per day
D. Assumed by local government circular

B. Based on output per day

The document that lists the description, quantity and rate of every item of work in a project is known as the
A. Specification
B. Bill of Quantities
C. Tender notice
D. Work order

B. Bill of Quantities

In the long wall-short wall method of estimation, the length of the short wall is taken as the centre-to-centre distance
A. minus the breadth of the wall
B. minus half the breadth of the wall on each side
Note: Options A and B are equivalent when the wall breadth is the same at both ends; the supplied key selects B.
C. plus the breadth of the wall
D. measured out-to-out

B. minus half the breadth of the wall on each side

The straight-line method of depreciation assumes that a property loses value
A. at an increasing rate every year
B. at a decreasing rate every year
C. by a constant amount every year
D. only in the final year of its life

C. by a constant amount every year

The value of a property arrived at by capitalizing its net annual income is obtained through the
A. book value method
B. rental (income) method of valuation
C. cost method of valuation
D. depreciated replacement method

B. rental (income) method of valuation

The rate analysis of a construction item generally includes the cost of materials, labour, and
A. contractor's overheads and profit
B. VAT only
C. cost of land
D. design consultancy fee

A. contractor's overheads and profit

In a detailed estimate, the provision for contingencies is, usually:
A. 1%
B. 3 to 5%
C. 10%
D. 12 to 15%

B. 3 to 5%

Annual rent is generally fixed at:
A. 1 to 2% of value of building
B. 2 to 5% of value of building
C. 5 to 10% of value of building
D. 10 to 25% of value of building

C. 5 to 10% of value of building

The two types of specifications commonly are:
A. initial and final specification
B. material and item specification
C. labour and equipment specification
D. none of the above

D. none of the above

The specification of work includes:
A. quality of materials
B. technology of work
C. measurement of work
D. all of the above

D. all of the above

Most important elements of the estimate are
A. rates
B. drawing
C. specifications
D. all of the above

A. rates

The quantities of wood work in a door frame having clear opening in the wall is 7' x 4' and chowukats are 4" x 3". The end of horizontal chowukats projection is 6" horns on each side into the wall is
A. 2 cubic feet
B. 3 cubic feet
C. 4 cubic feet
D. 5 cubic feet

A. 2 cubic feet

The main purpose of rate analysis is
A. to find actual cost per unit item
B. to find economical use of materials
C. both a) and b)
D. none of the above

A. to find actual cost per unit item

Of the total estimated cost of the building, the cost of electrification usually accounts for
A. 1%
B. 5%
C. 8%
D. 20%

C. 8%

A document containing detailed description of all items (not quantities) of work together with their current rates is called
A. tender form
B. schedule rates
C. analysis of rates
D. abstract estimate

B. schedule rates
