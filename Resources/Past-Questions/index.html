About these past questions

Practicing past exam questions is the most effective strategy for excelling in Nepal Engineering Council (NEC) license exams, Public Service Commission (PSC) Loksewa examinations, and Tribhuvan University (TU/IOE) semester exams. CivPrepMaster provides a well-organized collection of past papers across all 26 core civil engineering subjects, from Engineering Drawing and Strength of Materials to advanced topics like Hydropower Engineering and RCC Design.

The archive spans the entire BE Civil Engineering curriculum under IOE, Tribhuvan University. Whether you are preparing for second-semester basics like Fluid Mechanics and Surveying, or final-year specializations such as Irrigation Engineering, Airport Engineering, and Construction Management, you will find year-wise sorted question papers with solutions to maximize your exam readiness.
