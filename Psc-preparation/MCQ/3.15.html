A is the brother of B. C is the father of A. D is the brother of E. E is the daughter of B. Then, D is the _____ of A.
a) Son
b) Nephew
c) Cousin
d) Uncle

b) Nephew
Explanation: A is the uncle of D (since D is the son of B, and A is B's brother). Therefore, D is the nephew of A.

Pointing to a photograph, a man said, "I have no brother or sister, but that man's father is my father's son." Whose photograph was it?
a) His own
b) His son
c) His father
d) His nephew

b) His son
Explanation: Since he has no siblings, "my father's son" refers to himself. So, "that man's father is me," meaning the photograph is of his son.

If X is the brother of the son of Y's son, how is X related to Y?
a) Son
b) Brother
c) Grandson
d) Cousin

c) Grandson
Explanation: Y's son's son is Y's grandson. X is the brother of Y's grandson, so X is also Y's grandson.

A woman introduces a man as the son of the brother of her mother. How is the man related to the woman?
a) Nephew
b) Uncle
c) Cousin
d) Brother

c) Cousin
Explanation: The brother of her mother is her uncle. The son of her uncle is her cousin.

Looking at a portrait, a man said, "His mother is the wife of my father's son. Brothers and sisters I have none." At whose portrait was the man looking?
a) His son
b) His nephew
c) His own
d) His father

a) His son
Explanation: Since he has no siblings, "my father's son" is himself. "The wife of my father's son" is his wife. "His mother is my wife" means the portrait is of his son.
