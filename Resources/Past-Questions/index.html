02

About these past questions

Practising past exam questions is the most effective strategy for excelling in Nepal Engineering Council (NEC) license exams, Public Service Commission (PSC) Loksewa examinations, and Tribhuvan University (TU/IOE) semester exams. This page maps the 26 core civil engineering subjects — from Engineering Drawing and Strength of Materials to Hydropower Engineering and RCC Design — so you can see what each exam expects from every subject and plan your revision in syllabus order. Papers are added to each subject as they are collected and typed up; the count for a subject appears on its card.

The subject map spans the entire BE Civil Engineering curriculum under IOE, Tribhuvan University. Whether you are preparing for second-semester basics like Fluid Mechanics and Surveying, or final-year specialisations such as Irrigation Engineering, Airport Engineering and Construction Management, every subject is listed here with the exam pattern it belongs to. For timed practice today, use the NEC practice tests and PSC model exam sets; for theory, the subject notes are complete for Engineering Drawing and Construction Materials.

03

How to use these papers

Papers are grouped by subject, and each NEC License (ACiE) paper follows the ten chapters of the Nepal Engineering Council syllabus, so a single subject map works for NEC, PSC Lok Sewa and TU/IOE semester revision. Work through one subject at a time in this order:

  1. Read the question paper first, without solving it. Mark every question you recognise and every one you do not — the gap is your revision list.
  2. Write full answers under exam time (about one minute per objective question, and the stated marks for subjective papers) before opening any solution.
  3. Compare against the notes for that subject, then note repeated topics: NEC and PSC re-use the same core numericals — bearing capacity, rate analysis, float, discharge — year after year.
  4. Re-attempt the paper a week later. Only a second clean attempt shows whether the method stuck.

Start with the subjects that carry the most marks in your exam, then fill the gaps. The NEC notes hub and 600-point final capsule cover the theory behind these papers, and PSC practice tests add timed MCQ drills.

04

Past questions: common questions

Where can I download NEC License civil engineering past questions?
Every paper collected on this site is listed by subject on this page. Open a subject to see its questions; the NEC License preparation hub links the same papers by syllabus chapter.
Are TU, IOE and PSC papers the same as NEC License papers?
No, but they overlap heavily. NEC License (ACiE) papers follow the ten-chapter NEC syllabus, PSC Lok Sewa papers follow the Public Service Commission curriculum, and TU/IOE papers follow the semester syllabus. Solving all three is the fastest way to cover a subject.
Do these pages include solutions?
Solutions are published under Solutions and inside the subject notes. Where a worked solution is not online yet, the paper is still useful for timed practice.
How many questions are in the NEC License civil paper?
The NEC License objective paper is 100 multiple-choice questions in 2 hours, drawn from the ten syllabus chapters; the practical/interview stage follows the written paper.
