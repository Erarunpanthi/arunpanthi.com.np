What comes next in the sequence: A, C, F, J, ?
a) M
b) N
c) O
d) P

c) O
Explanation: The pattern is +2, +3, +4, +5 letters. J(10) + 5 = O(15).

Complete the series: B, D, G, K, ?
a) N
b) O
c) P
d) Q

c) P
Explanation: The pattern is +2, +3, +4, +5. K(11) + 5 = P(16).

Find the next letter: Z, Y, X, W, ?
a) U
b) V
c) T
d) S

c) V
Explanation: This is a reverse alphabetical sequence. W - 1 = V.

What letter comes next: A, E, I, M, ?
a) O
b) P
c) Q
d) R

c) Q
Explanation: The pattern is +4 letters. M(13) + 4 = Q(17).

Complete the pattern: AB, CD, EF, GH, ?
a) IJ
b) JK
c) HI
d) KL

a) IJ
Explanation: Each pair consists of consecutive letters, and each new pair starts with the next letter after the previous pair.
