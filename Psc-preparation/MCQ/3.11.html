Complete the series: 2, 4, 8, 16, ?
a) 20
b) 24
c) 32
d) 64

c) 32
Explanation: This is a geometric progression where each number is multiplied by 2. So, 16 × 2 = 32.

If BOOK is coded as CPPL, how is PEN coded?
a) QFO
b) QDO
c) ODM
d) QEN

b) QDO
Explanation: Each letter is shifted by +1, -1, +1, -1 pattern. P(+1)=Q, E(-1)=D, N(+1)=O. So, PEN becomes QDO.

Which word does not belong to the group?
a) Apple
b) Banana
c) Carrot
d) Orange

c) Carrot
Explanation: Apple, Banana, and Orange are fruits, while Carrot is a vegetable.

Find the missing number: 3, 6, 11, 18, ?, 38
a) 25
b) 27
c) 29
d) 31

b) 27
Explanation: The pattern is +3, +5, +7, +9, +11. So, 18 + 9 = 27.

A is the brother of B. C is the father of A. D is the brother of E. E is the daughter of B. Then, D is the _____ of A.
a) Son
b) Nephew
c) Cousin
d) Uncle

b) Nephew
Explanation: A is the uncle of D (since D is the son of B, and A is B's brother). Therefore, D is the nephew of A.

Choose the odd one out:
a) Triangle
b) Square
c) Circle
d) Rectangle

c) Circle
Explanation: Triangle, Square, and Rectangle are polygons with straight sides, while Circle is a curved shape.

If all Bloops are Razzies and some Razzies are Lazzies, then:
a) All Bloops are Lazzies
b) Some Bloops are Lazzies
c) No Bloops are Lazzies
d) None of the above

d) None of the above
Explanation: We cannot conclude that any Bloops are Lazzies. Some Razzies are Lazzies, but we don't know if those Razzies are also Bloops.

What comes next in the sequence: A, C, F, J, ?
a) M
b) N
c) O
d) P

c) O
Explanation: The pattern is +2, +3, +4, +5 letters. J(10) + 5 = O(15).

Mirror image of 'b' is:
a) d
b) p
c) q
d) b

a) d
Explanation: When 'b' is reflected in a mirror, it appears as 'd'.

If 5 workers can complete a task in 12 days, how many days will 10 workers take?
a) 4 days
b) 6 days
c) 8 days
d) 10 days

b) 6 days
Explanation: Workers and days are inversely proportional. 5 × 12 = 10 × x, so x = 6 days.
