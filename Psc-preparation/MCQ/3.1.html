Slope Deflection Method MCQ

Question: While using slope deflection method, in which direction is moment taken as positive?

a) Clockwise
b) Anti-clockwise
c) Depends upon case
d) Depends upon loading

a) Clockwise

Explanation: Clockwise moments are always taken as positive while using the slope deflection method. This sign convention is standard in structural analysis for consistency in calculations and equations. The slope deflection method is a displacement method used for analyzing indeterminate structures, and maintaining a consistent sign convention (clockwise = positive) is crucial for accurate results.

About Slope Deflection Method

The Slope Deflection Method is a fundamental structural analysis technique used for analyzing statically indeterminate beams and frames. This method was developed by George A. Maney in 1915 and remains an essential topic for Civil Engineering students preparing for PSC (Public Service Commission) exams and Nepal Engineering License examinations.

Key Points to Remember:

  • Clockwise moments are considered positive (+)
  • Anti-clockwise moments are considered negative (-)
  • This method uses joint rotations and displacements as unknowns
  • It is classified as a displacement method of structural analysis
  • Suitable for analyzing continuous beams and rigid frames

Why This Matters for PSC & License Exams?

Understanding the sign convention in the slope deflection method is crucial for solving structural analysis problems in competitive exams like Nepal Engineering Service (NES), Loksewa, and Nepal Engineering Council (NEC) License Exams. Questions on structural analysis frequently appear in these examinations, and mastering the fundamentals can significantly improve your score.
