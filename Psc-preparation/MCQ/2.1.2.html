If BOOK is coded as CPPL, how is PEN coded?
a) QFO
b) QDO
c) ODM
d) QEN

b) QDO
Explanation: Each letter is shifted by +1, -1, +1 pattern. P(+1)=Q, E(-1)=D, N(+1)=O.

In a certain code, CAT is written as DBU. How is DOG written?
a) EPH
b) FQH
c) EPF
d) FPG

a) EPH
Explanation: Each letter is shifted by +1. D→E, O→P, G→H.
