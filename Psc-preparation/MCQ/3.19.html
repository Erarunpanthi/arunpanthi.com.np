Mirror image of 'b' is:
a) d
b) p
c) q
d) b

a) d
Explanation: When 'b' is reflected in a mirror, it appears as 'd'.

What is the mirror image of 'L' when the mirror is placed vertically?
a) L
b) ⌃
c) ⌄
d) ⅃

d) ⅃
Explanation: When L is reflected vertically, it becomes a backward L (⅃).

Which letter looks the same in a mirror?
a) A
b) B
c) F
d) G

a) A
Explanation: The letter A is symmetrical vertically, so its mirror image looks the same.

If you hold up the word "AMBULANCE" to a mirror, what happens?
a) It reads the same
b) It appears reversed
c) It disappears
d) It becomes upside down

b) It appears reversed
Explanation: In a mirror, text appears reversed left-to-right. This is why ambulances have "ECNALUBMA\" written on them.

What is the water image (horizontal mirror) of 'M'?
a) M
b) W
c) E
d) 3

b) W
Explanation: When M is reflected horizontally (as in water), it appears as W.
