Public Service Charter

What is/are the Citizen's Charter(s)?
a) Commitment of the Organization
b) High-Standard Services
c) Service Providers towards providing quality
d) All of above

d) All of above
Explanation: A Citizen's Charter is a comprehensive document that includes:
- Commitment of the Organization: Public declaration of service standards and promises
- High-Standard Services: Specification of quality benchmarks for services
- Service Providers' Role: Clear identification of responsibilities of service providers
Additional elements: Service timelines, fees, grievance mechanisms, and contact information.

Which country is credited with the introduction of the Citizen's Charter?
a) United States
b) India
c) 1991
d) United Kingdom
e) Canada

d) United Kingdom
Explanation: The Citizen's Charter was first introduced in the United Kingdom under Prime Minister John Major in 1991. It aimed to improve public services by setting clear standards and ensuring accountability.

b) 1991

What is a Citizen's Charter?
a) A legal document
b) A set of commitments by public services
c) A financial plan
d) A political manifesto

b) A set of commitments by public services
Explanation: A Citizen's Charter is not a legal document, financial plan, or political manifesto. It is a formal declaration by public service organizations outlining their commitments, service standards, and responsibilities to ensure transparency, accountability, and quality service delivery to citizens.

The main aim of a Citizen's Charter is to:
a) Increase taxes
b) Improve public service quality
c) Regulate businesses
d) Enhance political campaigns

b) Improve public service quality
Explanation: The Citizen's Charter is designed to enhance transparency, accountability, and efficiency in public services. Its primary aim is to improve the quality of service delivery to citizens by setting clear standards, timelines, and grievance mechanisms.
